Drop pronunciation recordings here as MP3 files named after the Welsh word or phrase in
lower case, accents dropped, spaces and punctuation as hyphens, e.g. cymru.mp3, iechyd-da.mp3,
bore-da.mp3, gwyl-dewi.mp3, cofiwch-dryweryn.mp3.
The play buttons (Words to know, the word of the day and the phrases on welsh.html) use these
first, and fall back to the device's speech voice when a recording is missing.
Credit any recording made by someone else in AUDIO_CREDITS in src/cymraeg.py.
