    05 · Language

    Cymraeg, the oldest language of Britain

    Welsh grew out of Brittonic, spoken across most of Britain before the Romans. Its oldest poetry dates from around 600 AD, and it is still the everyday language of more than half a million people.

    Welsh speakers by census

    A century of decline, and a fight back
